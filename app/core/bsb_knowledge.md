## Calculation Logic (For Agent Internal Use)
- **Invoice Value** = (Material Rate * Qty) + 18% GST.
- **If Transport included**: Add Transport Rate + 18% GST on Transport.
- **Delivered Rate per kg**: (Total Material Cost + Total Transport Cost) / Quantity.
